About Our Municipality

Where the Mountains Meet the Sea

The Municipality of Real is a vibrant coastal town in the fourth district of Quezon Province, nestled between the majestic Sierra Madre mountain range and the vast Pacific Ocean. It serves as a major gateway along the eastern coast of Luzon.

Established in 1901, Real has grown into a bustling center for trade, fishing, agriculture, and ecotourism. The municipality is known for its rich biodiversity, thriving marine resources, and the warm hospitality of its people.
